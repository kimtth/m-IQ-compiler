# IT Consulting Industry

> [!abstract] Reference navigation
> IT consulting helps organizations plan, implement, and operate technology solutions.

## Connected concepts

- [[Topics/cybersecurity|Cybersecurity]]

---

IT consulting provides technical expertise and delivery support when an organization needs help changing its systems or ways of working.

## Common services

- **Advisory:** assess needs, risks, and possible approaches.
- **Implementation:** configure, integrate, test, and deploy systems.
- **Change support:** prepare processes and users for a new solution.
- **Managed services:** monitor, maintain, and support live systems.

## Typical workflow

1. Define the business problem and desired outcome.
2. Review the current environment and constraints.
3. Select and design an appropriate solution.
4. Implement, test, and release the solution.
5. Train users and provide ongoing support where needed.

## Delivery considerations

- Clear scope, ownership, and success measures reduce delivery risk.
- Security, privacy, reliability, and regulatory needs should be addressed throughout the work.
- Configuration is usually easier to maintain than extensive customization.
- Effective adoption depends on people and processes as well as technology.

## Basic measures

- Delivery progress against scope, schedule, and budget.
- System quality, reliability, and support outcomes.
- User adoption and satisfaction.
- Business results linked to the original objective.

## Terminology

- **Advisory:** guidance used to shape a technology decision.
- **Implementation:** work required to put a solution into use.
- **Integration:** connecting systems so they can exchange data or actions.
- **Managed service:** ongoing operation and support provided by another organization.
